Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services

How do I know which frame size is right for me?

We recommend using our Virtual Try-On feature to see how different frames look on your face. You can also refer to our Frame Sizing Guide, which includes measurements for each frame and tips for finding the perfect fit based on your face shape.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return your eyewear within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. The product must be in original condition with all packaging and tags intact.

Do you offer prescription lenses?

Yes, we offer prescription lenses for all our frames. During the ordering process, you can select the prescription option and upload your prescription details. Our team of opticians will ensure your lenses are crafted to perfection.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within Nigeria takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is also available for an additional fee. For international orders, delivery times vary by location but typically range from 7-14 business days.

Can I try frames before purchasing?

While we don't have physical stores, our Virtual Try-On technology allows you to see how frames look on your face from the comfort of your home. You can upload a photo or use your camera to try on different styles in real-time.
